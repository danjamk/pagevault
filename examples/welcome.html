What just happened

You ran make deploy and then make verify. Verify didn't just ping the Worker — it published this file as a public document over the API and printed the link you followed here. Publishing and reading are the same system, and you've now used both.

The one thing PageVault refuses to trust

Every document it serves is treated as hostile: LLM-generated, running its own JavaScript, possibly built from content the model never controlled. So it renders inside a sandboxed iframe with an opaque origin — sealed off from the page around it, from your session, and from the network. This very page is running under that seal right now. Where it goes from here

  • Connect Claude. The same deployment is a remote MCP server. Point Claude at /mcp with your bearer token and it can publish and search from the chat.
  • Put it on your domain. Re-run make setup and give it your hostname — still Public, the same documents, now on your own address.
  • Climb to Secured. Re-run setup and choose Secured: Cloudflare Access turns on, and documents can be gated to named email addresses. Client portals live here too, with permissions on the portal rather than the link. Your documents carry across untouched.
